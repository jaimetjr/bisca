# Priority note — read before adding any more locales

The app ships 12 UI languages, but Brisca/Briscola/Bisca is **not** played everywhere the app
can speak. Being indexed in a language and having demand in that market are different things.
The locales below are tiered by realistic expected return, not alphabetically:

- **Tier 1 (do these first):** Italian and European Portuguese. Briscola is played by
  "almost everybody" in Italy; Bisca is among Portugal's most played games after Sueca.
- **Tier 2 (cheap, marginal):** French, German, Dutch, Russian — largely diaspora demand.
- **Tier 3 (completeness only):** Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Arabic. Effectively nobody
  searches for this game in these markets. Add them because a listing costs nothing, but do
  not expect installs and do not spend time tuning them.

## Known copy caveat — Italian
The Italian `home.subtitle` in `shared/i18n/translations.ts` used to read "Il Classico Gioco di
Carte Spagnolo", telling Italian players that Briscola is a *Spanish* card game. It now reads
"Il Classico Gioco di Carte Italiano". The fix reaches players only in a new app build; the
store listing itself was never affected.

The card artwork is Spanish-suited (`assets/images/spanish`) with no Italian deck option. The
Spanish and Italian 40-card decks are structurally identical and the app already localizes the
suit names to Denari/Coppe/Spade/Bastoni, so this is an aesthetic mismatch rather than a
correctness problem — but it is the most likely source of Italian one-star reviews.
