# Localization — English [en-US]

## App name (max 30)
Brisca — Spanish Card Game

## Short description (max 80)
Play Brisca, the classic Spanish 40-card game — vs AI or online, 1v1 & 2v2.

## Full description (max 4000)
Brisca is the beloved Spanish trick-taking card game, now on your phone — free, offline, and ad-supported.

Play the classic 40-card Spanish deck (Oros, Copas, Espadas, Bastos) against smart AI opponents or challenge friends online. Win tricks, collect points, and be the first to 61 to take the game.

FEATURES
• Play vs AI — three difficulty levels, 1v1 or 2v2 team play
• Play online — real-time 1v1 and 2v2 multiplayer with room codes
• Learn in seconds — a built-in tutorial walks you through the deck, card points, the trump suit, and how tricks are won
• 12 languages — English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, German, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Arabic, Russian, and Dutch
• Track your progress — stats, daily quests, and achievements (with a free account)
• Play offline — full single-player against AI with no connection needed
• Clean, authentic Spanish deck artwork

HOW TO PLAY
Each player gets 3 cards and one card's suit becomes the trump for the whole game. The highest trump wins a trick; with no trump, the highest card of the led suit wins. Aces are worth 11 points, Threes 10, Kings 4, Knights 3, and Jacks 2 — 120 points in the deck, and 61 wins.

Whether you grew up playing Brisca with family or you're learning for the first time, jump in and play a hand.
